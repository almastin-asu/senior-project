Random Encounters

Need a random encounter to throw at your players? Click the dice to get a prompt!

Strange Cemetery

You come across an ordinary looking cemetery. You see some strange movement and take a closer look just to see three turkeys circling a headstone to the point that there is a path made in the dirt around the stone. The stone itself seems normal enough, the only notable feature is the word HARRISON across the middle. Investigating may lead to an undead attack, a turkey attack, or maybe it really is just a weird occurance.

Geese...?

You find a large pond in a clearing in the forest. The water is perfectly still yet the honking of the 50 geese surrounding the pond ruins the tranquil setting. They don't seem to notice you but interacting with the pond or attacking a goose triggers combat. You'll find that the water holds something worse than a simple goose...

Why is it always goblins?

You hear a hushed conversation coming up in front of you. You can't make out the full sentences but hear the words "ambush" and "attack" repeated several times. There's the sound of a branch snapping and the voices go silent. Four armed goblins surround you intending to attack but maybe they can be talked out of it?

Blackout Riddle

Suddenly you lose all vision, almost as if someone put a blindfold on you. A raspy voice speaks directly in your ear but there is nothing physically there. It says "I am tall when I am young and short when I am old. What am I?" When the correct answer is guessed (a candle), vision returns as if nothing ever happened.
